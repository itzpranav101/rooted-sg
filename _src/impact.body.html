Why Singapore first

Start small. Start here.

01The gap is measured here.

We surveyed 3,043 Singapore residents. The need is real and we can show it.

02Land is tight, imports are high.

About ninety percent of food is imported, so every household pot counts toward 30 by 30.

03A year-round growing season.

Heat and humidity mean plants grow all year, and stress and disease show up fast. A good test bench.

04Neighbours are next door.

Blocks, corridors and shared gardens make sharing the harvest the natural next step.
