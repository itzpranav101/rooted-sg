The app · iOS

A garden that keeps count.

The Rooted SG app is the counting half of Meco (Measure + Eco). Garden, Market, Quests and BioDex. Everything on this page is a real screen from the build, recorded on an iPhone.

01 — Index

Five places. One count.

Today, Garden, Market, Quests, BioDex. The line at the bottom is the whole idea: don't count what we plant, count what we keep alive.

02 — Garden

Kept alive for 194 days.

Pandan has 12 verified check-ins and a care streak of five. Survival plus recovery from a struggling period is what makes it a Legacy Plant.

03 — Market

Offer it, or ask a neighbour.

The Exchange lists offers, wanted, free and seeds, sorted by what's nearby. Singapore grows under 10% of what it eats; every household plot counts toward the 30-by-30 goal.

04 — A real listing

Cherry tomatoes off the corridor rack.

About 300 g a week, free to a neighbour, pick-up from the block on weekend mornings. The grower's record shows 421 plant-days.

05 — Profile

Level 6. 314 plant-days.

Lifetime plant-days, XP, check-ins and milestones from First Plant to 7-Day Care, 30 Days, 90 Days and Veteran.
