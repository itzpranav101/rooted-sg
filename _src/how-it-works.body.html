The 46-byte frame

Small enough for Bluetooth. Strict enough to trust.

Every reading travels in one little-endian frame. A CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE covers bytes 0 to 43. Absent sensors use sentinels (0xFFFF, 0x8000, 0xFF) instead of fake zeros, and the Swift and Kotlin encoders are tested to be byte-identical.

The tier rule

Every metric says how real it is.

BUILT

You can trust the number

Soil moisture and conductivity, air temperature, humidity and pressure, light. Printed, wired and logged.

PROTOTYPE

Read it as a direction

Stem impedance and the twin-pump loop. They work on the bench; impedance is reported as trend and timing, never a calibrated figure, and never a stress the soil and air can't corroborate.

ROADMAP

Simulated, and labelled

Nitrate, phosphate, potassium and sap flow. The UI never shows these as measurements; they come from a simulator and say so. Electro-culture ships off, logged as an experiment.
