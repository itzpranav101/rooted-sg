How it works

Measure. Act. Check it worked.

That last step is what makes the rest honest. If the moisture didn't recover, the app says so instead of dosing again.

The closed loop

It starts at the floor. It stops at the target.

Two different numbers, so it can never chatter on and off around one threshold. An eleven-second cycle: the reading falls to the floor, both impellers spin up, the streams merge at the Y, water leaves at the nose, and the reading climbs back.

01

Sense

Moisture, conductivity, air and light, stamped with the time.

02

Decide

Compare to the crop's own band, not a universal number.

03

Deliver

Twin pumps, one Y, one bore to the nose. Every dose is an event with a volume.

04

Verify

If moisture didn't recover, say so.

One stake, a whole block

A fungus needs weather, not luck.

Hours of high humidity, a temperature band, a canopy that stays wet. Those conditions are shared across a block, so one well-placed station speaks for the plants around it. Several stakes turn a number into a map.

What it does not do

The needles read the plant they are in. They don't sense a neighbour's tissue or locate a fungus. A block warning comes from shared weather, and is raised only when two kinds of evidence agree.

Privacy, enforced twice

A block is only described when at least 3 stakes from at least 2 accounts have reported, checked in the API and again in the database view.

The 46-byte frame

Small enough for Bluetooth. Strict enough to trust.

Every reading travels in one little-endian frame. A CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE covers bytes 0 to 43. Absent sensors use sentinels (0xFFFF, 0x8000, 0xFF) instead of fake zeros, and the Swift and Kotlin encoders are tested to be byte-identical.

The tier rule

Every metric says how real it is.

BUILT

You can trust the number

Soil moisture and conductivity, air temperature, humidity and pressure, light. Printed, wired and logged.

PROTOTYPE

Read it as a direction

Stem impedance. It works on the bench; we report trend and timing, never a calibrated figure, and never a stress the soil and air sensors can't corroborate.

ROADMAP

Simulated, and labelled

Nitrate, phosphate, potassium and sap flow. The UI never shows these as measurements; they come from a simulator and say so. Electro-culture ships off, logged as an experiment.
