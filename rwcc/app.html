The role of AI

Helpful, honest and private.

On-device inference

Processed on the phone, at zero marginal cost per photo.

Confidence thresholds

Low-confidence results ask for a retake.

Cloud AI is opt-in

Off by default. Only a resized photo is sent.

PDPA by design

Neighbourhoods only, never exact locations.

AI supports Rooted SG, but it isn't the whole product. It checks photos, powers the Plant Doctor, suggests species, and answers questions as Mojo.

01 · Apps

Two native apps, one data model

  • SwiftUI
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Feature parity

iPhone and Android do the same thing the same way. 43 iOS and 46 Android automated tests keep them level.

02 · Computer vision

Image processing on the phone

  • OpenCV
  • Vegetation coverage
  • Perceptual hashing
  • Colour · fullness · light

Each photo is checked for living plant cover and compared with the last one, so an old or repeated photo cannot earn a day.

03 · Models

Open models, with a human in the loop

  • Hugging Face
  • On-device inference
  • Confidence thresholds
  • "AI suggested" labels

Low-confidence results ask for a retake. Species suggestions stay labelled until someone confirms them.

04 · Backend and privacy

Minimal by design

  • Backend API
  • Opt-in cloud AI
  • PDPA by design
  • Resized photos only

Cloud AI is off by default. Only a resized photo is ever sent. Locations stay at neighbourhood level. 70 backend tests pass.

05 · Statistics and simulation

Python for the numbers

  • Monte Carlo
  • Triangular sampling
  • Spearman rank correlation
  • Wilson score interval
  • Pareto analysis

The 100,000-run forecast above is a Python script in the repository. Survey results carry confidence intervals, and the survival rate comes with a Wilson interval.

06 · How we make it better

What the pilot feeds back in

  • Replace every assumed range with a measured one, then re-run the simulation.
  • Use consented pilot photos to tune the confidence threshold, so fewer honest check-ins ask for a retake.
  • Watch the retake rate and how often people overrule the Plant Doctor.
  • Keep a weekly control chart on survival and publish the result either way.
In detail

Every method and model, by name.

Image processing (OpenCV)
Colour-space conversionBGR to HSV and LabSeparates colour from brightness, so yellowing reads the same in sun and in shade.
Excess Green indexExG = 2g − r − bScores how green each pixel is, to find the living plant in the frame. [10]
Otsu thresholdingAutomatic cut-offPicks the green versus not-green threshold for each photo on its own. [9]
Morphology and connected componentsOpening, closing, labellingRemoves speckle and measures canopy cover, the "fullness" clue.
Hue-band shareHSV yellow to brown rangeMeasures the share of leaf area that has gone yellow or brown.
Luminance histogramV channel, mean and spreadThe "light" clue: is the plant getting enough?
Laplacian varianceBlur detectionRejects a blurry photo before it is judged.
Perceptual hashDCT pHash and Hamming distanceCatches a repeated or re-used photo, so one picture cannot earn two days. [11]
Models (Hugging Face)
Mobile classifiersMobileNetV3, EfficientNet-LiteSmall enough to run on the phone. e.g. timm/mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k, google/mobilenet_v2_1.0_224. [12]
Server-side baselineResNet-50For comparison only: microsoft/resnet-50. [13]
Accuracy ceilingVision Transformer ViT-B/16Reference for the best a larger model can do: google/vit-base-patch16-224. [14]
Zero-shot promptsCLIP ViT-B/32Species and symptom prompts without retraining: openai/clip-vit-base-patch32. [15]
Training dataPlantVillage, Pl@ntNet-300KPlant disease and species images used to fine-tune the classifiers. [16][17]
On-device deploymentInt8 quantisation, Core ML, TensorFlow Lite, ONNXShrinks the models and runs them locally on iPhone and Android.
Confidence calibrationTemperature scaling, thresholdsMakes confidence honest, so a low score asks for a retake. [18]
Ask MojoRetrieval-augmented generationAnswers grounded in your own garden's records. [19]
Python, for testing and prediction
Monte Carlo simulation100,000 runs, triangular inputsThe year-three forecast. [1]
Latin Hypercube samplingStratified drawsCross-checks the sampling. [2]
Sobol indicesPick-freeze estimatorsWhich input explains the spread. [3]
Bootstrap500 resamplesInterval on the median. [4]
Wilson score intervalBinomial proportionsHonest intervals on small and measured samples. [5]
Spearman rank correlationSensitivity rankingHow strongly each input moves the result. [6]
Pareto analysis, 5 Whys, pre-mortem, control chartQuality toolsFinding the cause, and watching the pilot. [7][8]
