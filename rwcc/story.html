01 · WhenEvenings, weekends, holidays

Weekday evenings after homework, Saturdays and Sundays, and school holidays. Late nights before deadlines.

02 · AlongsideSchool and exams

We paused the build for exams, teammates covered each other, a small daily routine kept it moving, and we planned big pushes around the calendar.

03 · HowFive people, three areas

App design and code. Research and survey. Community and outreach. Each owned by someone.

04 · The costWhat we gave up

Sleep, time with friends and family, hobbies and sports, and rest in the holidays.

The hardest part was a build that kept failing, until it didn't.

What kept us going: seeing a plant survive, the community growing, and each other.
