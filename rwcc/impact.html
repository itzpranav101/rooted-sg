The simulation

Chance the pilot clears

100,000 simulated outcomes of year three · extra plants alive at 12 months · a forecast, not an outcome

01 · What is most likely

About 1,460 more plants alive. Most runs beat 1,000 (72%), but only about 1 in 3 (35%) reach our base case of 1,814. We treat 1,814 as a target, not a promise.

02 · What decides it

The twelve-week pilot. In about 14 of every 100 runs it misses +5 points, so we stay at two schools and redesign. In the other 86, we scale to 20 schools, then more.

03 · What we will do

Run the pilot first: two schools, 240 students. Measure uptake in week one and survival at twelve weeks, and publish the result either way.

Evidence

How we will know whether it actually worked.

Primary measure · 12-month plant survival

A control chart, not a single number: we plot weekly survival with three-sigma limits, so a real improvement is told apart from noise. An example of the shape. The pilot has not run yet.

Secondary measures

Check-in rate per active user per week, 28-day retention, and time from problem flagged to problem fixed.

Our stopping rule

If 12-week survival is not at least 5 pp above the 18% baseline, we do not scale. We redesign, and say publicly that it did not work.

Why this is credible

Every Plant-Day is already verified by the app: a real plant, a fresh photo, the right day. The measurement instrument is the product.

Sustainability

What keeps it alive after the competition.

Keeping people using it

The streak only grows while a plant is alive, so stopping costs something. Mojo asks once a day, never twice. Leagues reset weekly, so a bad week is survivable.

Designed against the 20.2% who lose motivation
Light enough to last

Every recognition model runs on the phone, so the service does not get heavier as more people join. The backend holds counters and photos, nothing more.

No per-user inference load
Going without us

Schools own their own leagues, and Roots is neighbour to neighbour. A school can run it without us.

No single point of failure that is a person
