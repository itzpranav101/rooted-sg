Pareto

What the vital few told us to build
  1. 1 · Forgetting and no time41.8% → Mojo's daily nudge and the check-in streak
  2. 2 · Nobody to ask22.3% → the Plant Doctor, and neighbours on Roots
  3. 3 · Losing motivation20.2% → Plant-Days, leagues and visible small wins

The rest are resource problems: space, cost, pets. We deliberately did not build for them.
