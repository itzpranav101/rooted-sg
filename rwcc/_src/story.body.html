What we believe

Four rules we hold ourselves to.

01 · Count

Only what is alive counts.

A Plant-Day needs a real plant and a fresh photo. It cannot be bought, back-dated or faked.

02 · Honest

Say what is proven, and what is not.

We keep a plain list of what the pilot must still show, and we publish the result even if it says we are wrong.

03 · Careful

Design the mistakes out.

Checks run quietly in the background, so the right thing is the easy thing. Every fix gets checked again.

04 · Patient

Small first, then the island.

One class, then sixty schools. We grow only as far as the evidence reaches.

The time it took

Nine months of evenings and weekends, on top of everything else.

0

Nobody did all of it. Everybody did a part.

01 · WhenEvenings, weekends, holidays

Weekday evenings after homework, Saturdays and Sundays, and school holidays. Late nights before deadlines.

02 · AlongsideSchool and exams

We paused the build for exams, teammates covered each other, a small daily routine kept it moving, and we planned big pushes around the calendar.

03 · HowFive people, three areas

App design and code. Research and survey. Community and outreach. Each owned by someone.

04 · The costWhat we gave up

Sleep, time with friends and family, hobbies and sports, and rest in the holidays.

The hardest part was a build that kept failing, until it didn't.

What kept us going: seeing a plant survive, the community growing, and each other.
