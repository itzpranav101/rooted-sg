01 · The garden

Every plant has someone looking after it.

Singapore plants millions of them. Almost nobody can see which ones are still alive.

02 · One plant

Zoom in on one plant, then one leaf.

Rooted SG starts where you are: a single plant, and the phone in your hand.

03 · The leaf

A leaf is a city of living cells.

Under a few centimetres of green, thousands of cells are working together.

04 · The cells

Walls, nuclei and the chloroplasts that make it green.

While the cells are healthy, the leaf looks the way you expect.

05 · The fungus

A spore lands. Hyphae grow through the tissue.

Thin threads push through the cell walls at pits and reach the leaf vein.

06 · Underneath

Cells fail long before the leaf looks wrong.

The plant plugs its own pipes to fight back, and the green starts to fade.

07 · On the surface

Then the colour changes: yellow, then spots.

This is the first moment anyone can see it, with their own eyes or a camera.

08 · The camera

Your phone sees what the surface shows.

The Plant Doctor reads colour and fullness from a photo and turns them into clues.

09 · The call

The AI finds clues. You make the call, and act early.

A short plan, a check-in tomorrow, and the day counts if the plant is still alive.
