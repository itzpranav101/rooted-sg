Meet Meco

Meco is the stake and the app, as one thing.

RootStake module+Rooted SG app=Meco

Meco is our name for the two halves sold and used together: the RootStake module that measures the plant, and the Rooted SG app that decides what to do and checks that it worked.

In the bundle

The house module, the soil probe with its hidden 4.5 mm tube, the stem electrode clip, the wire and tubing between them, and the Rooted SG app on your phone.

Why together

The app is only as honest as its sensor, and a sensor with no app is just a thermometer. Meco closes the loop: measure, decide, water, verify.

Prototype — Stem electrode

Ask the stem, not the leaf.

A sprung clip closes on the stem and drives two titanium needles through the outer tissue and into the conducting core. The clip is printed and wired back to the house module.

1 kHz — around the cells

Low frequency goes around.

A small alternating current is swept between the needles. At 1 kHz it can't cross a living membrane, so it threads the wet space between the cells.

100 kHz — through the cells

High frequency goes through.

At 100 kHz it passes straight through the membranes. The ratio between the two is the measurement. On the bench we report a trend and its timing, never a calibrated figure.
