Prototype · Stem electrode

Ask the stem, not the leaf.

A sprung clip closes on the stem and drives two extremely thin titanium micro-needles through the outer skin into the conducting tissue. It sends a tiny alternating current and measures how easily it flows. The clip is printed and wired back to the house module.

1 kHz · around the cells

Low frequency goes around.

A small alternating current is swept between the needles. At 1 kHz it can't cross a living membrane, so it threads the wet space between the cells.

100 kHz · through the cells

High frequency goes through.

At 100 kHz it passes straight through the membranes. The ratio between the two is the measurement. On the bench we report a trend and its timing, never a calibrated figure.

The system

The app and the stake are one loop.

A reading leaves the stem, crosses the soil, rides 46 bytes of Bluetooth to your phone, becomes a verdict, and comes back down as water and feed through a bore in the probe. Then the app checks it worked.
